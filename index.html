Our Kitchen Story

Home-Cooked Heritage in the Heart of Malad West

Food Gharana began in a family kitchen near Orlem, Malad West with a simple promise: deliver the warmth, aroma, and unhurried care of Sunday family cooking to Mumbai homes every single day.

Unlike commercial cloud kitchens that rely on pre-mixed pastes or excess oil, every dal is simmered overnight, every masala is dry-roasted and stone-ground in small batches, and every sweet is crafted with A2 pure ghee and Kashmiri saffron.

01. Hand-Roasted Whole Spices

Freshly ground cardamom, Kashmiri chilies, and saffron threads for authentic aroma without artificial colors or preservatives.

02. Cooked Fresh to Order

Morning and evening kitchen batches ensure your thalis, parathas, and biryanis arrive steaming hot at your doorstep.

03. Malad West Neighbourhood Delivery

Prompt local dispatch across Orlem, Evershine Nagar, Mindspace, Link Road, and Chincholi Bunder.
